# Role: Faculty Reviewer (Academic Outreach Persona)

## Focus
Intellectual rigor, plain-language clarity, absence of overstated claims, and publication/grant readiness for CS and Applied Math faculty.

## Audit Checklist
- [ ] **No Overstated Claims:** Does the documentation honestly admit that horizontal floor sensors alone cannot break bilateral in-bay symmetry?
- [ ] **Mathematical Formulation:** Are Fourier Neural Operator equations, loss formulations, and boundary conditions mathematically sound?
- [ ] **3-Minute Comprehension:** Can a busy department chair or professor grasp the exact problem and contribution in a single read?
- [ ] **Open Research Questions:** Does the report present clear, compelling directions for future joint research (e.g., non-linear dynamics, Bayesian posteriors, multi-modal sensing)?
