## Claim Classification Legend
- **`OBSERVED`**: Direct numerical measurement from frozen execution logs and JSON artifacts.
- **`EMPIRICALLY_SUPPORTED`**: Hypothesis or trend statistically substantiated across controlled experimental matrices.
- **`MECHANISTIC_INTERPRETATION`**: Plausible physical or optimization explanation consistent with observations (gradient norms, variance, loss formulations).
- **`LIMITATION`**: Explicitly documented boundary condition, idealization, or non-modeled physical phenomenon.
- **`FUTURE_WORK`**: Stated prospective research trajectory (strictly framed as not yet performed).
